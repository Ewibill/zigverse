The Session Score

Perform each movement while watching the meters. Press C during each one — every capture downloads a JSON; drag them all into the chat and Glyph calibrates the engine to YOUR rig.

1 · Silence. Loops off, horn down, room quiet. Watch: floor readout. It should sit near 0. If body idles above 0.05, the chain hums — tell Glyph.
2 · The tone ladder. One SWAM voice at a time (bass clarinet, then tenor): long tones — low, middle, high register — each soft, medium, full. Watch: body should ride your dynamics without pinning 1.00 (if it pins, lower the M2 gain a touch); shine should CLIMB with register.
3 · The bellows. One long crescendo–decrescendo from nothing to everything and back. Watch: body should be a smooth hill, no jumps, no clip flag.
4 · Ribbons vs tongues. Your fast ribbon playing, then hard tongued attacks. Watch: ribbons should barely move bite; tongues should SPIKE it. If ribbons fire strikes, the threshold needs your signature.
5 · Vibrato & growl. Held note with deep vibrato; then growl if the patch does it. Watch: vibrato ≈ no bite. Growl should raise shine/body texture.
6 · The pedal. Hold one note, sweep the Mission pedal full range, slowly. Watch: whichever meters move — that's your pedal becoming a VISUAL dial. This is new territory: the pedal never existed in MIDI.
7 · History alone. Start an RC-600 loop, hands off the horn. Watch: this is what your PAST reads as. Then play over it — live + history superposed.

The split (leverage)

The M2 has two inputs. Feed input 1 = the UltraLite mix (loops/history) and input 2 = your live line (presence), then arm SPLIT here: L and R become two independent voices. That's the routing that lets worlds tell your past from your present — no software needed, one cable move.
