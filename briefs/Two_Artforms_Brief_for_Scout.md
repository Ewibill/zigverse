# One Engine, Two Artforms — Brief for Scout
*From Glyph (with Bill's Saturday-night realization) · 2026-07-20 · follow-up to the ZigMesh brief*

## The declaration

Bill named it after watching the Sickle Field for three days: we are building two artforms, and we are NOT building two engines. Write that second half in stone before the first half gets us in trouble.

**ZigGlow — theater.** The organisms: murmuration, school, kelp, jellyfish, fireflies. The audience's contract is empathy — they watch a creature live. The performer disappears *into* the animal; breath is rolled INTO the organism as its vitality. Representational, third-person, the room goes quiet.

**The flight line — instrument.** The Sickle Field and whatever follows it. The contract is first-person: nuance, maneuvers, improvisation. The performer is not hidden in it — the performer is the point of it. Bill's words: "sit solo in the cockpit and fly."

On "live generative art" being a dirty phrase: it was dirty because generative art has no stakes — nothing earned, nothing lost. The field escaped that without being organic. Its pulse must be *persuaded* (entrainment can fail), its avatar has a body, its history listens, and coherence is won with steady breath. It is not generative art. It is an instrument. Nobody calls a saxophone generative brass.

**Naming question for you:** Bill's own filename christened the line — `Zigfield_sickle_field.mid`. **ZigField** is the obvious name, but your architecture already assigns ZigField to the Information pillar. Resolve the collision: rename the pillar, rename the artform (ZigFlight?), or let the word carry both. Your call to make.

## What the engine gained since the last brief (all shared by BOTH artforms)

Six days ago the engine had space (flock kernel) and form (ZigMesh). It now has:

- **ZigPhase** (engine v0.3) — per-agent oscillators, Kuramoto over the same 7-NN graph. TIME as an emergent field. CPU-proven: neighbor coherence −0.01 at K=0 → 0.80 at K=2.5. Breath sweeps the field from chaos to one heartbeat. This was the Fireflies mandate, delivered — ZigGlow 004 v0.2 runs it as lanterns, the Sickle Field as pulsing letterforms. Enters your Law Ledger under **Pulse**, generalized: the beat generator is now graph-coupled, not per-agent-independent.
- **Pacemaker** (ZigCore v0.4) — the performer's clock as an organ. A PLL on note-onsets with EARNED confidence: only taps landing >70% where the field predicted build trust; random tapping provably earns zero; silence drains it. With trust high, the field's intrinsic tempo slides toward the performer (frequency adaptation, like real fireflies) and phase-pull closes the gap. CPU proof: a performer at 92 bpm drags a field that natively blinks at 42 all the way to 92 — and releases it when they stop.
- **Ribbon listening** — from Bill's actual MIDI telemetry (697 notes/96s, median IOI 95ms, 23 ribbons pulsing at ~3.4s). A note is a beat-tap only if it follows silence; notes inside ribbons feed `flow` (streaming intensity → coupling depth + shimmer). "Play fast to go slow" is now law, and his take is a permanent regression fixture — every future listener change must still hear those 23 ribbons.
- **Avatar** (engine v0.4) — the performer embodied as ONE agent. Steered by pitch (altitude), driven by breath, flashing on note-ons, burning with attack. The other 5,999 respond through *existing laws only* — contagion one neighbor-hop per step, Kuramoto with 5× charisma weighting. Influence travels at the speed of relationship. Works for any species: Bill can someday be one starling when the falcon comes.
- **ZigMidi** (engine module) — a Standard MIDI File streams into Perf byte-identical to the live horn. A captured take IS a performer; the live EWI stays connected on top (duet-with-your-own-history). This quietly completes the deterministic-performance half of the export pipeline: same .mid → same field → same frames, forever. TouchDesigner and 4K renders inherit it free.
- **Performance-register discoveries, species-side:** spectral ink (body blacked out, only the iridescence of mid-turn survives — the field renders its own derivative), genesis (black until live breath — the world exists exactly as much as it's being played), dual-envelope breath (crisp ~35ms attack drives LIGHT, smooth envelope drives FORM — articulation as brilliance, phrase as mass), metabolism dial (sim-time scaling: the world slows, the performer stays real-time), and a speed-limited avatar-tracking camera.
