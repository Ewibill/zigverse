# The Zigverse Codex
*The operating system. Adopted 2026-07-23 from Scout's Four Pillars. This is the compass — Scout, Glyph, and Bill share it.*

## The shift
We are no longer asking the engine to create scenes. We are asking it to **accumulate natural laws**. Before any code is written, every project answers one question:

> **What reusable principle are we adding to the universe?**

If a project only makes a beautiful result, it ends with that project. If it contributes a law, every future organism inherits it. That — not the count of ZigGlows — is the measure of progress. The living tally lives in the engine itself: `ZigCore.Canon` (guarded by `test/canon_ref.mjs`). The universe can state itself.

## The Prime Law — Inevitability
Nothing exists merely because it looks good. Everything is inevitable because of the world it inhabits. "Why is the organism blue?" is never answered "because blue looked nice." It is answered "because this habitat filters long wavelengths," or "because that material scatters light this way," or "because that species evolved under those conditions." Color, form, and motion are **consequences**, not decisions. We don't choose backgrounds; we understand habitats, and color emerges.

## The Four Pillars (they flow downward)
**PHYSICS → HABITAT → LIFE → EXPERIENCE.** Nothing flows backward.

1. **ZigPhysics** — the immutable rules. Gravity, buoyancy, diffusion, inertia, elasticity, viscosity, attraction, repulsion, conservation, turbulence — and *criticality* (Load). Never artistic; they simply exist. *We have 6.*
2. **ZigHabitat** — the environment. Habitats don't invent rules, they inherit them and express them differently. A reef and a desert obey one gravity. **This is our thinnest pillar — the frontier.** *We have 3.*
3. **ZigLife** — species. Organisms are adaptations to habitats, not decorations. *We have 5.*
4. **ZigExperience** — the observer as an environmental force. The breath, the music, the presence, the attention become part of the ecology. This is where Zigverse is unlike anything else. *We have 8.*

## What makes nature compelling: coherence, not complexity
Everything belongs; nothing is arbitrary. Inevitability is how we buy coherence. And the sweet spot of organization where a living thing feels most alive — poised between slack and rigid — is **Load** (Bill's law, now in Physics). Real murmurations hold themselves exactly there. Every dial we build has a Load sweet spot; only a performer's ear can find it.

## The Surface Principle (rendering · adopted 2026-07-23)
Surfaces should never appear mathematically generated. They should feel weathered, grown, layered, deposited, eroded, hydrated, crystallized, or woven — with richness that keeps resolving at every zoom (organic fractals), yet always **subordinate to behavior**. We learn from Refik Anadol's strength — making digital matter feel physically present — without imitating his look; our lane is that richness *wedded to purposeful biological behavior*, driven by breath. Under Inevitability this is not decoration: the weathering verbs are **Habitat acting on Life over time** — the coral is pitted because the reef eroded it. Detail that means something recedes when the eye is needed elsewhere (governed by Load). First rung built on the Material Studio bench (fBm micro-surface, wheel-zoom); carrying it into the field engine is the next expedition.

## Expeditions replace ZigGlows
A project is no longer a scene — it is an **Expedition** with two outputs:
- **Scientific output** — what new law entered Zigverse? *(permanent — enters the Canon)*
- **Artistic output** — what organism first revealed that law? *(its first expression; the HTML is the last page, not the first)*

Every expedition has a paper hidden inside it, for us, not the audience. Open the folder and the story reads top-down: Research → Habitat → Physics → Materials → Behavior → Performance → Camera → Implementation → Final HTML.

## The pipeline
Phenomenon → Research → Extract Laws → add to ZigPhysics → generate Habitat → Species evolves → Performance Mapping → Camera Language → Experience → Ship. That isn't a pipeline; it's evolution.

## Roles
- **Scout** leads expeditions: researches phenomena, extracts universal principles, defines coherent habitats.
- **Glyph** implements: turns principles into elegant, performant, *proven* systems inside the pillars. The implementation target is ZigPhysics / ZigHabitat / ZigLife / ZigExperience — never a single ZigGlow. Iron rules unchanged (tests green in/out, bundles-only to chat, opt-in splices, CPU proofs before glass).
- **Bill** curates the canon: directs, judges what truly feels alive, decides which discoveries belong.

## How Glyph receives an expedition
Not "build a Crystal Forest." Instead:

```
Zigverse Expedition NNN
Subject:        <phenomenon>
Discoveries:    <the principles observed in nature>
ZigPhysics +:   <new immutable rules>
ZigHabitat +:   <new environment>
ZigLife +:      <species the habitat affords>
Engine +:       <the concrete solver/field/parameter Glyph adds>
```

Glyph's answer is never just the demo — it's the expanded Canon. The demo is the first proof the law works.

## The measure of progress
Not the number of ZigGlows. The **richness of the universe from which all future ZigGlows can emerge.** After fifty expeditions, ZigCore should hold not fifty finished scenes but fifty new ways for life to emerge.
