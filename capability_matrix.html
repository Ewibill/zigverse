PHASE 1 — behavior & matter

Teach ZigCore how things behave and what they are made of. Close it not with more organisms, but by maturing the individual-behavior axis and the material optics until each is a reusable component. We are here.

PHASE 2 — environment

Teach ZigCore where things live. The environment becomes the protagonist; organisms become the instruments that let a person perceive the invisible law. Begins only when Phase 1's vocabulary is rich enough to inhabit a world.

Every completed capability is added to the permanent engine. A specimen is spent; a capability compounds.
